This is geometry/pitch evidence, not complete visual or pixel parity.
The plain 8x8 repeat rounds to 18x13 DIP; Spotify's 12x6 chart rounds to
27x10 DIP. Their row-step rounding and across-band phase differ by design.
D2D and CoreGraphics differ in curve coverage, stroke AA and image interpolation;
pixel identity is neither expected nor used as an acceptance criterion.
Native linear bitmap sampling at +/-90 degrees differs from the horizontal
sample by at most 1/255 per channel (measured); tests enforce this limit.
Nearest sampling at matched tile/output scale is tested for exact rotation.
Unmatched nearest scales can put sample coordinates on texel ties, so their
rotations are not used as a pixel-identity criterion. PNG stores straight alpha;
WIC unpremultiply/re-premultiply rounding changes at most 1/255 per channel,
with unchanged alpha (measured and enforced).

Native placement contract: contentInsetPixels=ceil(bandDip*deviceScale).
Supported logical-to-physical scale range is deliberately1/8x..8x. Finite but
near-zero positive scales are rejected before physical-to-DIP division. Derived
DIP dimensions and piece offset/extents are also validated before integer casts;
tests cover deviceScale1e-38f with physical1000x241 and the supported minimum.
Optional physical content dimensions avoid physical->DIP->ceil off-by-one.
An optional authoritative physicalPaddingPixels accepts the native geometry's
double-precision padding when conversion to the source float band/scale crosses
an integer. It must be within one pixel of the renderer's natural padding.
Tests cover band10.00000001 at 1x: float source band remains10, native padding11
creates a transparent one-pixel gutter rather than stretching yarn.
The fractional difference between the integer canvas padding and true logical
band is transparent coverage gutter; it does not move the target content.
For 620x140 DIP at 1.25x the integer canvas is 801x201, inset13, while the
actual band remains 12.5 physical pixels. At 2x the fixture remains 1280x320.
Tuck is preserved under the content; the host must exclude content in its
compositor clip or place decoration below its target. PNGs do not emulate this
live compositor policy. No live host wiring or desktop parity is claimed here.
